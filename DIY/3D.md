# Reachy Eyes — 3D Printing

> **Scope:** This page covers 3D printing only. Assembly, electronics, and firmware are covered in the separate companion guides.

These parts are designed to be quickly printed on any generic basic modern printer. The thinness is intentional to save weight.

Note that many cities have readily available 3D printers available. Check local libraries, makerspaces, and other creative venues. If you are really stuck, you can have the part 3D printed by a 3rd party and shipped to you.
